Dear Ms. Torres,

This letter constitutes a **Formal Quality Notice** under Section 8.3 of the Master Supply Agreement between Northfield Automotive Corporation ("Northfield") and Precision Dynamics LLC ("Supplier"), executed in 2021 and most recently amended the previous fiscal year.

### Quality Performance Summary

Northfield's incoming inspection team has identified a sustained upward trend in defect escape rates on shipments received from Precision Dynamics under Supplier Code 4417 (Automotive Components — drivetrain sub-assemblies, sensor housings, and precision brackets). The relevant performance data is as follows:

| Period | Lots Received | Lots Rejected | Defect Escape Rate | Contractual Threshold |
|--------|--------------|---------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Q1 (prior year) | 847 | 12 | 1.42% | 2.00% |
| Q2 (prior year) | 891 | 15 | 1.68% | 2.00% |
| Q3 (prior year) | 863 | 21 | 2.43% | 2.00% |
| Q4 (prior year) | 912 | 28 | 3.07% | 2.00% |
| Q1 (current year, partial) | 614 | 22 | 3.58% | 2.00% |

The defect escape rate has exceeded the contractual threshold of **2.00%** for two consecutive quarters and continues to trend upward. The most commonly identified defect modes include dimensional nonconformance, surface finish defects, and intermittent failures under vibration testing.

### Contract Value and Scope

The current Master Supply Agreement covers the supply of automotive components across three vehicle programs. The annual contract value is approximately **$45 million**, encompassing:

- Drivetrain sub-assemblies (Program NX-400): ~$22M annually
- Precision sensor housings (Program NX-520): ~$14M annually
- Structural brackets and mounts (Program NX-650): ~$9M annually

Precision Dynamics is currently designated as a **sole-source supplier** for two of these three programs. Northfield has identified qualified alternate suppliers for all three programs as part of standard supply chain risk management.

### Required Actions

Under the terms of the Master Supply Agreement, Northfield requires the following:

**1. Containment (Immediate — within 10 business days)**

Precision Dynamics must implement immediate containment measures on all automotive shipments, including 100% outgoing inspection on the identified defect modes. Containment activities must be documented and communicated to Northfield's Supplier Quality team.

**2. Root Cause Analysis (Within 30 days)**

Precision Dynamics must submit a formal root cause analysis using an industry-standard methodology (8D, fishbone, or equivalent). The analysis must address:

- Why defect escape rates have increased over the past four quarters
- Whether the root causes are isolated to specific production shifts, equipment, operators, or material lots
- Whether the issues affect only automotive components or extend to other product lines

**3. Corrective Action Plan (Within 45 days)**

Precision Dynamics must submit a comprehensive corrective action plan that includes:

- Specific process changes to address identified root causes
- Updated control plans and inspection protocols
- Statistical evidence that corrective actions are effective (SPC data, capability studies)
- A timeline for full implementation

**4. Sustained Performance (Within 90 days)**

Precision Dynamics must demonstrate a return to sustained performance **below the 2.00% defect escape rate threshold** within 90 days of this notice. Performance will be evaluated based on a rolling 30-day defect escape rate calculated from Northfield incoming inspection data.

### Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to meet the 90-day performance requirement will result in:

- Escalation to **Probationary Supplier Status** under Northfield's Supplier Rating System
- Initiation of **qualification activities for alternate suppliers** on all three vehicle programs
- Potential invocation of **contract termination provisions** under Section 12.1 of the Master Supply Agreement, with a 180-day transition period

Northfield values the partnership with Precision Dynamics and prefers to resolve this matter collaboratively. However, the quality requirements of our vehicle programs — and the safety expectations of our end customers — are non-negotiable.

I will be visiting the Precision Dynamics facility in late spring to conduct an on-site quality audit and review the corrective action implementation. Please coordinate scheduling through my office.
