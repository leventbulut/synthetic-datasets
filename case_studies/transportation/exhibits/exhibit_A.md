# Exhibit A — Client Notification Letter

---

**RIDGELINE RETAIL**
*Office of the Vice President, Logistics & Supply Chain*

---

**FROM:** David Hargrove, Vice President of Logistics
**TO:** Nina Volkov, Director of Fleet Operations, Apex Logistics
**CC:** Thomas Brennan (CEO, Apex Logistics); Sandra Cho (Chief Procurement Officer, Ridgeline Retail)
**DATE:** January 9
**SUBJECT:** Formal Notice of Contract Termination — Delivery Performance Failures and Conditions for Reinstatement

---

Nina,

I want to be direct. This letter serves as Ridgeline Retail's formal notification that we are terminating our logistics services agreement with Apex Logistics, effective end of the current quarter. I called you this morning because I believe you deserve the courtesy, and because I want you to understand that this decision was not made lightly.

We have been partners for four years. For most of that time, Apex met or exceeded our performance standards. That is no longer the case, and the deterioration during the holiday season has forced our hand.

**Performance Summary — Last Four Quarters**

Our service-level agreement specifies a 92% on-time delivery rate across all priority tiers. Here is what we observed:

| Quarter | On-Time Rate | SLA Target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Prior Year) | 91.3% | 92.0% | ⚠️ Below SLA |
| Q2 (Prior Year) | 89.7% | 92.0% | ⚠️ Below SLA |
| Q3 (Prior Year) | 85.2% | 92.0% | ❌ Critical |
| Q4 (Prior Year — Holiday) | 78.4% | 92.0% | ❌ Contract Breach |

The trajectory speaks for itself. We granted a grace period after Q1, accepted your remediation plan after Q2, and escalated to executive review after Q3. The holiday quarter was the breaking point.

**The Holiday Corridor — What Happened**

Between late November and late December, Ridgeline shipped approximately 38,000 packages through Apex's network. Of those:

- **Same Day priority:** On-time rate fell to 61%. Our customers were promised same-day delivery. Nearly four in ten packages arrived late — some by hours, some by a full day. The customer complaint volume for this tier alone exceeded the prior three months combined.
- **Express priority:** On-time rate dropped to 74%. These are customers paying a premium for speed. A 26% failure rate is not a service — it's a liability.
- **Standard priority:** On-time rate was 82%. Even the lowest-cost tier failed to meet the 92% SLA.

**Weather is not an excuse.** We understand that the holiday quarter brought adverse conditions — snow events in the northern corridor, fog in the coastal routes. Every carrier faces weather. What we cannot accept is a partner that has no system for predicting which shipments are at risk before they leave the warehouse. When a snowstorm is forecast and your dispatch system sends a standard van on a 200-kilometer highway route with six stops, that isn't bad luck. It's a planning failure.

**The Cost to Ridgeline**

I want to be specific about what this has meant for us:

- **Customer satisfaction:** Our Net Promoter Score dropped 11 points in Q4, with delivery reliability cited as the primary driver in post-purchase surveys.
- **Refunds and credits:** We issued approximately $420,000 in customer refunds and promotional credits directly attributable to late deliveries through Apex's network.
- **Brand damage:** We received 2,300 social media complaints mentioning late delivery during the holiday period. We don't have the luxury of explaining to our customers that the logistics partner failed — in their eyes, Ridgeline failed.

**What It Would Take**

I told Sandra Cho that I would include this section against my better judgment, because I respect what Apex built and I know this isn't the company you intended to run. If Apex can demonstrate the following within sixty days, Ridgeline will consider reinstating the contract on a twelve-month probationary basis:

1. **A working pre-dispatch prediction system** that can flag at-risk shipments by delivery priority, route type, and weather condition — with demonstrated accuracy across all three tiers. We don't need perfection. We need a partner who can tell us, before the truck leaves, that a shipment has a 35% chance of delay so we can reroute, reprioritize, or set customer expectations.

2. **A credible data quality remediation plan.** During our joint review in Q3, your analytics team acknowledged issues with your TMS data — negative delay values, inflated stop counts, impossible driving durations. If your prediction system is built on unreliable data, it will produce unreliable predictions. We need to see that the foundation is sound.

3. **A dynamic cost estimation capability.** Your current flat-rate pricing doesn't reflect actual delivery costs, and both of us are losing money as a result — you on underpriced routes, us on overpaying for routes that should cost less. A cost model tied to actual route characteristics would benefit both parties.

I want to be clear: this is a conditional offer, not a guarantee. If Apex builds the system and it works, we'll talk. If not, we wish you well and will have completed our transition to an alternative carrier by end of quarter.

You have my direct number. I hope you'll use it.

— David Hargrove
