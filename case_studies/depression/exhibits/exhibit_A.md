Dear Dr. Vasquez,

This letter serves as formal notification that BrightPath Community Mental Health Network's Medicaid § 1915(b) waiver is scheduled for renewal review on **September 15, 2025**. As part of the Bureau's Behavioral Health Modernization Initiative, the renewal process for FY2026 includes updated performance and technology requirements for all participating provider networks.

### Background

BrightPath's current waiver covers outpatient mental health services for approximately **45,000 unique patients annually** across your network of **twelve (12) clinics**. The Bureau recognizes BrightPath's significant role as a safety-net behavioral health provider in the region. Medicaid reimbursement constitutes approximately 60% of BrightPath's operating revenue, and continued waiver authorization is essential to the network's financial sustainability.

In reviewing BrightPath's most recent annual performance report, the Bureau notes the following:

- **Patient Volume:** 400,000 screening records in the current data repository (Jan 2022 – Sep 2024)
- **Clinical Acuity:** Network-wide mean PHQ-9 score of **10.3** (moderate depression range), indicating a high-acuity patient population consistent with safety-net provider status
- **Comorbidity Burden:** Network-wide mean GAD-7 score of **7.2** (mild-moderate anxiety), suggesting significant co-occurring anxiety in the depression treatment population
- **Severity Distribution:** Approximately **52.7%** of patients classified as None/Mild severity, **19.0%** as Moderate, and **28.2%** as Severe
- **Treatment Outcomes:** Mean recovery score of **76.3** (median **85.4**), indicating that while the majority of patients show adequate recovery, a meaningful subgroup demonstrates persistently poor treatment response

### Renewal Requirements

For FY2026 waiver renewal, the Bureau requires participating networks to demonstrate the following capabilities:

**1. Validated Severity Classification System**

The network must present a data-driven model capable of classifying incoming patients into severity tiers (None/Mild, Moderate, Severe) based on intake screening data. The Bureau expects:

- Model performance metrics demonstrating clinical reliability
- Analysis of model performance across patient subgroups (demographic, socioeconomic)
- Documentation of the model's sensitivity for identifying Severe cases, where misclassification carries the highest clinical risk
- A plan for ongoing model validation and recalibration

**2. Predictive Recovery Model**

The network must demonstrate a predictive tool that estimates patient recovery trajectory at or near intake. This model should:

- Identify patients at elevated risk for poor treatment response or early disengagement
- Quantify the key drivers of recovery outcomes
- Distinguish between modifiable factors (e.g., treatment engagement, social support) and non-modifiable factors (e.g., demographic characteristics, clinical history)
- Include performance benchmarks (RMSE, MAE, R², or equivalent)

**3. Quality Improvement Plan**

The network must submit a written plan describing how the severity classification and recovery prediction systems will be integrated into clinical workflows. The plan should address:

- How model outputs will be communicated to clinical staff
- What clinical actions will be triggered by model predictions
- How the network will monitor for algorithmic bias or unintended disparate impact
- Patient notification and consent protocols for predictive analytics
- A timeline for implementation and evaluation

### Data Quality Standards

The Bureau is aware that BrightPath completed an EMR migration in July 2023. We expect the network's submission to include a transparent accounting of data quality issues encountered during analysis, including the nature and extent of missing data, the strategies used to address it, and any limitations these issues impose on model reliability.

Provider networks that cannot demonstrate adequate data quality controls may be asked to implement a remediation plan before full waiver renewal is granted.

The September 15 review panel will include:

The Bureau encourages BrightPath to prepare materials that are accessible to all panel members, including those without technical data science backgrounds.
