Based on your hospital's Excess Readmission Ratios (ERRs), the following payment adjustment will apply to all Medicare discharges beginning **October 1, 2025**:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Payment Adjustment Factor** | 0.9700 |
| **Effective Penalty** | 3.00% reduction |

This adjustment applies to all Medicare inpatient prospective payment system (IPPS) base operating DRG payments. The penalty is not condition-specific; it reduces reimbursement across all Medicare admissions.

## Section 3: National Context

The Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program was established by the Affordable Care Act in 2012. As of FY2025, approximately 2,500 hospitals nationally face readmission penalties. The maximum penalty is 3% of Medicare base operating DRG payments.

Key considerations for Lakeview:

- **Penalty calculation is relative.** Lakeview's readmission rates are compared to expected rates based on patient case mix. Improving absolute readmission numbers matters, but so does understanding how Lakeview's patient population compares to national norms.

- **The penalty applies to all Medicare discharges.** Even a 1% penalty rate, applied across thousands of Medicare admissions, generates millions in lost revenue.

- **Evidence-based interventions exist.** Transitional care programs, post-discharge telephone follow-up, medication reconciliation, and community health worker programs have all demonstrated readmission reductions in peer-reviewed literature. The question is not whether solutions exist but which ones are cost-effective for Lakeview's specific patient population.

## Section 4: Quality Improvement Committee — Action Items

The following items were identified during the Quality Improvement Committee meeting of January 13, 2025:

1. **Data Audit.** Commission a comprehensive audit of the 500,000-record EHR extract. Assess data quality, identify patterns in missing values, and evaluate whether the 2023–2024 staffing crisis introduced systematic documentation gaps.

2. **Predictive Modeling.** Develop a readmission risk prediction model capable of stratifying patients at discharge. The model must be clinically interpretable, operationally actionable, and ethically defensible.

3. **Cost Analysis.** In parallel, develop a cost prediction model to support the CFO's budget planning process. Identify high-cost patient profiles and assess whether cost and readmission risk are correlated.

4. **Ethical Review.** Engage the hospital Ethics Committee to review the use of insurance status and socioeconomic proxies in predictive models. Develop a policy position before model deployment.

5. **Board Presentation.** Prepare a comprehensive presentation for the March 2025 Board of Directors meeting. Include model findings, implementation plan, resource requirements, and projected ROI of a readmission reduction initiative.
